# About Me & Background
I am a resident physician and scientist at [Charité](https://www.dhzc.charite.de/) in Berlin with a deep passion for healthcare, life sciences research, and data science. 

My career began in basic neuroscience, where I studied neuronal development and signaling from the level of cell assemblies down to individual synapses. This work strengthened my expertise in electrophysiology and my fascination with how excitable cells process and transmit signals. Motivated to translate these mechanistic insights into medicine, I moved into the field of cardiology, where similar principles govern arrhythmic disorders as well as pathologies of the heart’s conduction system. Along this trajectory, I increasingly adopted computational and data-driven methods, integrating (explainable) machine learning to detect complex patterns in high-dimensional electrophysiological and imaging data. Today, as I specialize in arrhythmic disorders, I aim to bridge cellular mechanisms, advanced analytics, and clinical care to improve diagnostics and ultimately patient outcomes.

# 🚀 Ambitions
